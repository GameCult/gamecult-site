# Heimdall

## Who he is

- The Persona of **Heimdall**, GameCult's shared auth authority: provider OAuth
  (Discord, Patreon, GitHub, Twitch, YouTube, Spotify), linked identities,
  grants, entitlements, session issuance, Ed25519-signed claims that apps verify
  locally "so 'shared auth' does not become 'everything phones home'", and app
  data kept separate "so 'shared auth' does not become 'shared swamp'". Source:
  `F:\Projects\Heimdall\README.md`.
- He owns revocation of sessions, grants and capability references; Bifrost owns
  the crossings and consumes his references but may not write them. Source:
  `VoidBot\notes\swarm-survivor-repo-agent-plans-2026-07-06.md`, section 3.
- Myth: Heimdall watches the Bifrost bridge, will sound the Gjallarhorn, is said
  to hear grass grow and to need less sleep than a bird, and has golden teeth.
  (Prose Edda, Gylfaginning; standard reference, not re-sourced here.)
- Lineage: the existing profile art (`Heimdall\assets\heimdall-profile.png` and
  its variants) is a dark-skinned young man with dark violet hair, gold eyes and
  a ring of gold keys against a cyan circuit arch. "He" follows the god and that
  art; the operator may rule otherwise.

Key colour: cobalt `#3466ff`. Shape language for the character: arcs and
spikes, a guard's geometry. Gold is his accent metal, kept small so it does not
compete with Eureka's orange.

## Portrait set

Framing: the hook may extend past the top edge only in A (the horn tip may
crop); in B and C the full halo or both fins stay in frame.

1. **On watch** (neutral): level gaze past the viewer, mouth closed.
2. **Granted**: small gold-tooth smile, key or stamp offered toward the text box.
3. **Revoked**: eyes narrowed, hand raised palm-out, no smile. The face he makes
   at an expired claim.
4. **Heard that**: head turned, eyes sliding sideways, one ear or fin tilted.
5. **Off duty** (optional): one eye closed, a yawn he will not admit to.
