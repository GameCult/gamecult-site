# VoidBot

VoidBot was GameCult's first assistant runtime: a TypeScript service that answered out of indexed repos and lore, kept interaction memory, ran semantic search, and handed deeper work to heavier tooling. It started as a Discord-facing bot. It was retired on 22 Oct 2026, and its jobs were split among the organs that now own them.

## Where The Work Went

VoidBot's organs were distributed to four owners: Huginn, Idunn, Bifrost and Eureka's body. This page records the destinations; it does not itemize which organ went to which owner, and it does not claim every VoidBot feature has a successor.

- **Huginn** owns semantic retrieval and typed-state inspection. Decisions, rulings and open work are queried as typed state in the mind. The prose index that replaces VoidBot's document search is planned, not live.
- **Idunn** is GameCult's deployment and daemon-survival authority.
- **Bifrost** governs topics, work items, receipts and reward pressure.

Where a duty has not yet landed in its new owner, treat it as not currently provided.
