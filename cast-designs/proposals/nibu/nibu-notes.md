# Nibu: SVG proposals, round 1

## Rebased on canon

Nibu already has an established design: `gamecult-site\GameCult\static\interactive\cotsc-praxis\character-sheets\nibu-sheet.png`
(also `cotsc-praxis\nibu.png`, `sprites\nibu\*` and `AetheriaLore\.voidbot\voice\nibu.png`). The coordinator relayed
the operator's ruling that all three directions keep that canon, and that canon overrides the brief's palette rules.
So the three directions in `../../nibu.md` (Figurehead, Smeared Hostess, Hull Hood) were dropped as identities.
Every sheet keeps the same Nibu: long lavender hair, the big crescent swoop over her near eye, the pink ribbon bow
on her far side, the pink circuit hair clip, violet eyes, the purple coat with circuit-glyph trim, the throat
frill, the cyan shoulder port and the glitch. Only the hook changes. The brief's B hook (the out-of-sync duplicate)
became this round's B.

`nibu-old-B.svg` is an unfinished draft of the pre-canon Smeared Hostess (bob and pillbox cap). It was never
rendered or reviewed, and is kept only because the coordinator asked for old-direction sheets to be kept.

Shared construction, drawn by hand: three-quarter view facing screen right, light from the upper left, a violet rim
on the far jaw and the coat, one shadow tone and one highlight per material, and an outline in `#2a1452` about twice
the weight of the inner lines. Canon palette: key `#9a6bff`, hair `#b9aef5`, coat `#4a2d8f`, line `#2a1452`,
ribbon `#f08cc0`, glitch/HUD cyan `#7af0ff`, skin `#f0d3dd`. The pink and cyan are near Kiko's and Aqua's hues;
each stays under about 10 percent of the figure.

## A: Source Slate (`nibu-A.svg`)

- **Design:** the lore steward with a cracked holo-slate raised beside her face. A SOURCE? header, a struck-through
  claim and a big pink question mark are on it. Her head tilts back, the near brow arches, her pupils slide toward
  the claim, and the mouth sneers at one corner. This is the "Source?" portrait from the set.
- **Silhouette:** the slate is a hard rectangular tab off the lower right, under the bow. At 48 px it reads as
  "girl holding a sign".
- **Changed after looking:** first pass held the slate inside the hair line, so it disappeared in the silhouette,
  and the glove read as a black blob. Moved the slate out past the hair, angled it outward, gave it a sleeve rising
  from the frame edge and fingertips over its bottom edge, made it opaque, and lowered the bust to fill the card.
- **Round 2:** the slate is a prop, so every expression must hold it or the hook disappears. Test a smaller,
  clip-on version for Docked and Grudging. Add more visible damage to the slate (the junkyard hull).

## B: Glitch Twin (`nibu-B.svg`) — strongest

- **Design:** canon Nibu, calm and half-lidded. Behind her and up to the left is a scanline hologram of herself
  from a failed timeline, scowling. One slice across her near eye has slipped to the twin's angry eye, boxed in a
  cyan glitch frame. That is the reset smear, made visible in a single neutral portrait.
- **Silhouette:** a stepped double crown, two heads where there should be one. It is the only cast silhouette of
  this kind.
- **Changed after looking:** pass 1 put the twin straight behind her, so only its hair showed, and the
  bleed-through bands ran across both of her eyes, which looked like a smeared visor. Raised and rotated the twin
  so its crown steps clear and its eye shows past her hair. Cut the bleed down to one slice over the near eye. Raised
  the tint's contrast. Moved the swoop out so it covers only the outer corner of the eye. Put a plate behind the
  nameplate, which the twin now crosses.
- **Round 2:** the twin's angry face is still mostly hidden behind her hair. Try mirroring the offset, or a larger,
  fainter twin looming above, so its expression reads at 128 px. Define how the slice changes per expression; in
  Recognition the twin should react first, as the brief says. Watch the stacked translucency at small sizes.

## C: Console Ring (`nibu-C.svg`)

- **Design:** she is the ship's controls. A segmented cyan HUD ring orbits her at a tilt, passing behind her head
  and in front of her collar, with glyph tiles riding on it. The eyes reflect cyan, and she gives a smug fanged
  smile. This is the "control is hers" reading.
- **Silhouette:** loops stick out from both sides of a long-hair mass. At 48 px it reads as an orbit, but thinly.
- **Changed after looking:** the first ring was too small and sat at the neck like a hula hoop or necklace. Made it
  larger and thicker, tilted it 15 degrees, lowered it so the front arc clears the chin, and scaled the bust so both
  ends fit inside the card.
- **Round 2:** the ring is the weakest silhouette of the three. Try a thicker band, or a second, partial ring.
  The front arc competes with the chest panel, so drop or simplify the panel in this direction.

## Known shared weaknesses (all three)

- The chest holo panel reads as a sticky note; push it toward the canon's softer blue bodice.
- The full-body chibis are simple lock-the-design figures (about 2.3 heads), not finished turnarounds.
- The near eye sits close to the swoop, so the face's left half is busy. The canon accepts this, but Docked may
  want the swoop pulled back one notch.
