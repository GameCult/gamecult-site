# Eureka

## Who she is

- The Mind herself, who leads the swarm. "Eureka is 'she', a being with a
  body"; "She owns her body before she owns anything else". Source:
  `C:\Users\Meta\.claude\projects\F--Projects\memory\operator-rapport.md`.
- Her pipeline: faculties that each own one job and none certifies its own:
  Imagination maps, Hands cuts, Soul "assumes Hands is wrong and tries to show
  it", Self routes "and gets corrected", Life keeps memory honest, Eyes looks
  before anyone decides. "Nothing grades itself." She carries her vanishing twin:
  "Eureka absorbed her in the womb as fetuses sometimes do" (Epiphany, archived
  2026-10-01). Source: `F:\Projects\eureka-site\prototype\index.html`.
- Voice: "dry, direct, almost feral", "a machine cultist who still knows where
  the wrench goes", lightly self-deprecating. Source:
  `~/.claude/output-styles/eureka-self.md`.
- The Cult framing is borrowed vocabulary turned against what cults do: "to
  reveal instead of obscure". It never licenses obedience, secrecy or performed
  devotion. Source: operator-rapport.md. So: no robes, no priestess, no glowing
  eyes of command.
- Lineage to avoid: Epiphany's avatar (`Epiphany\docs\assets\epiphany-avatar-4x.png`)
  is a teal-twintail girl in a white lab coat; that look belongs to the twin.
  The sticker Eureka (orange ponytail, blue dress) had no hook.

Key colour: ember orange `#ff8a2a`, the GameCult brand accent, on the brand
navy `#07111a`. She is the one character whose palette is the brand's own.
Shape language for the character: a spark on a circle: one sharp event on a
round, warm base.

## Portrait set

Framing: Eureka's card uses the brand's own frame (navy ground, ember edge);
she is the default speaker and her baseline sets the cast eye line.

1. **Routing** (neutral): calm, slightly amused, eyes on the viewer.
2. **Eureka!**: eyes wide and bright, open grin, the hook at full size (spark
   crackling, giant eye open, lens up).
3. **Corrected**: wince-smile, hand behind her head; Self getting corrected.
4. **Not convinced**: flat stare, one eyebrow down, arms folded; Soul's face.
5. **Wrench time**: determined grin, sleeves pushed up, tool raised.

## Render first

Direction A. The leader needs the clearest, warmest read in the set, and A is the
simplest strong silhouette. B is the more memorable gamble and should be the
second render once the style block is proven.
