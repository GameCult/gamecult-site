# GCLP

*"One commit, one README, one very large idea refusing to die politely."*

GCLP is the original public stub of the GameCult Labor Platform. The repo is almost comically small. The idea inside it was not. This is the public fossil of the studio's long-running attempt to drag work assignment, patronage, contributor history, and payout logic out of folklore and into software.

## Founding Idea

The founding idea was already the right one: a studio should not run on memory, vibes, and whoever still remembers what got promised in chat. The labor platform concept wanted a real structure for members, work, governance, patronage, and compensation years before the current buildout had the time or infrastructure to make it real.

## Trajectory

The public repo itself does not have much of one. It is a single initial commit with a tiny README. That sounds almost insulting until you remember that some ideas spend years in the "one obvious paragraph" stage before they finally earn an implementation budget.

The important trajectory happens after the public repo goes quiet. The concept resurfaces later as a more serious application effort with a real stack, a real deploy target, and a less romantic relationship to spreadsheets. In other words: GCLP is the seed. Bifrost is the part that grew a body.

## Ambition

The ambition never got smaller. If anything, it got less naive. GCLP wanted a studio operating system. Bifrost still wants that. The difference is that the newer effort knows which parts need boring software, which parts need policy, and which parts should not be allowed to touch money until they have earned some adult supervision.
