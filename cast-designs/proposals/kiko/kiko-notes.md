# Kiko: proposal notes, round 1

## The brief changed: canon wins

Kiko already has an established design: the praxis character sheet at
`F:\Projects\gamecult-site\GameCult\static\interactive\cotsc-praxis\character-sheets\kiko-sheet.png`
and its sprites in `sprites\kiko\`. It shows white-silver hair, white fox ears, a
white kitsune mask worn on the crown, round hair clips with gold bells and
magenta tassels, cyan eyes and a fang grin, a black coat trimmed in magenta and
cyan with a gold bell at the throat, several fluffy white tails, and a streaming
setup of monitors and stickers. `kiko.md` missed it. Its A/B/C (Pixel Tails,
Screen Mask, Hood Gremlin) were new designs. Here they are replaced by three
takes on the canon design. Each take turns up a different canon element as the
hook:

- **A, Seven Tails:** the tails.
- **B, The Mask:** the mask.
- **C, On Air:** the streaming gear.

Things that stay the same across all three: the face construction, cyan eyes,
hair, ears, bell clips and coat. That way the three read as one character.

`kiko-old-A.svg/.png` is the only sheet drawn from the old brief (Pixel Tails,
before the canon note arrived): magenta eyes, a black hoodie, and tails in four
event colours. It is kept for comparison. The old B and C were never drawn.

Palette for all three: magenta `#ff3fa4` (key, trims, tail tips), line
`#4a0f2e`, white `#f7f4ff` (hair, fur, mask), cyan `#26e0ff` (eyes, inner trim,
screen), coat `#1b1530`, gold `#ffcf3a` (bells). Skin is `#f2c4a8`. The skin
swatch is left off the sheet because the swatch grid holds six.

StreamPixels' stepped-pixel shape language is kept, but it lives in the
details. It is never the rendering style. It shows up as the staircase tail
tips in A, and as the pixel heart and cat stickers and the LIVE tag in C.

## A: Seven Tails

- **What it does.** Seven tails fan out behind her head. They are fluffy white
  with lilac shadow, and each tip is a magenta staircase. In silhouette the fan
  becomes a spiky crown with ears and mask on top, the most distinctive shape of
  the three at 48 px. The mask rides small on the crown. The expression is an
  open fang grin.
- **Changed after looking.**
  - The first canon pass hid the mask behind the bangs, so the mask now draws
    last.
  - The tails were angled so they clear the hair mass on both sides; before
    that, five of the seven sat invisibly behind the head.
  - The tip steps were refined (grid down to 70 percent of the tail grid), and
    the bust was scaled down so the fan fits inside the card.
  - The neck was cut down, because the neck shape was painting over the coat
    collar.
- **Push in round 2.**
  - The staircase tips still read as coarse blocks where tails overlap. Try
    fewer, larger steps, plus a thin white gap between neighbouring tails.
  - The white tails sit against lilac back hair with too little separation.
    Push the hair shadow cooler or give the tails a cyan rim.

## B: The Mask

- **What it does.** The kitsune mask is drawn at about 1.75 times A's size,
  tilted on the crown. It stacks a second pair of fox ears above her own, so
  the silhouette is tall and four-pointed. The painted mask face (closed
  happy eyes) works as a second expression beside her cat smile. A magenta
  cord with a large bell streams back from the mask.
- **Changed after looking.** The mask was enlarged and the cord ribbons were
  added, to pull the silhouette away from C's. The lone tail was removed from
  the bust, because only its stepped tip showed and it read as a glitch blob.
- **Push in round 2.**
  - At 48 px, B's silhouette is the closest to the other two: a tall blob with
    ears. The mask needs a stronger tilt or offset so it breaks the head outline
    sideways, not just upward.
  - Try letting the mask's painted face change per expression, as the
    portrait-set gag.

## C: On Air

- **What it does.** She leans out of a stream monitor. This is the canon
  overlay pose, cropped as a boxy screen behind her head with stickers and a
  LIVE tag on the bezel. She wears a magenta headset with sticker earcups and a
  boom mic jutting past her cheek, the mask pushed to the far side, and a cable
  curling down like a tail. In silhouette the screen makes a rectangle with ears
  breaking out of the top, which nothing else in the cast does.
- **Changed after looking.**
  - Headset and mic alone left the silhouette identical to B's, so the monitor
    was added behind her as the shape carrier.
  - The mic was pushed forward past the face contour.
  - A stray inner glyph on the far earcup was replaced with an LED ring and a
    pixel-cat sticker.
  - The LIVE lettering was redrawn.
- **Push in round 2.**
  - The far earcup is crowded by the far ear and the mask. Simplify to one of
    those two.
  - The monitor bezel loses its stickers behind the hair. Move them to the top
    corners.
  - The full-body sheet does not show the monitor yet.

## Strongest

C is the strongest. It is the most canon-faithful, its boxy silhouette is
unique in the cast, and the gear tells you what StreamPixels is. A is the
safest iconic read at thumbnail size.
