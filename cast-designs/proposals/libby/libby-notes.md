# Libby: SVG proposals, round 1

## Change of brief: canon overrides libby.md

Libby already has an established design: the cotsc-praxis character sheet
(`F:\Projects\gamecult-site\GameCult\static\interactive\cotsc-praxis\character-sheets\libby-sheet.png`,
sprites in `sprites\libby\`). It has long wavy lavender hair with a swept fringe
that hooks into a curl, round thin glasses, a crossed-bone hairpin, a black
ribbon bow with diamond motifs at the back of the head, small gold drop
earrings, a high-collared dark violet robe with mauve trim and diamond motifs, a
gold brooch with a violet gem, and glowing tomes, a skull stamp and skulls as
props. The coordinator ruled that canon overrides the brief, its palette rule
included. Libby stays lavender/violet, and the three directions in `libby.md`
(card-catalogue hat, mesh-weaver braids, reading-lamp shade) are dropped. A, B
and C are now three takes on the canon design. Each one changes the hook
emphasis, the prop and the framing for a Persona-style chibi bust. All three
are drawn fresh from the reference, mirrored to face screen right. None is
traced.

`libby-old-A.svg` / `.png` is the one sheet drawn from the old brief (the
card-catalogue hat, second pass) before the ruling. It is kept for the record.
It is not a candidate.

Canon palette used in all three: hair `#a99be0`, line `#2a1d40`, robe
`#241a33`, skin `#f6d6c4`, gold `#c8a24a`, glow `#b77cff`. Each material has one
shadow tone and one highlight tone. The rim light is lavender.

`libby-gen.py` assembles the three sheets from hand-written path strings. Change
it and rerun it rather than editing the SVGs directly.

## A: The Glowing Tome ("Found it")

- **What it does:** the canon idle and research pose as a bust. Open delighted
  smile, both brows up, an open tome held at chest height with violet glyphs
  and light rays rising off the pages. The hook emphasis is the hair: the swept
  fringe, the hooked curl, and the full wavy mass.
- **Changed after looking:** the first fringe read as a bowl cut with scalloped
  tips. It was redrawn as strands that sweep diagonally, each tip pointing down
  toward the face side, and the curl was made larger. The back hair outline got
  outward wave lobes, because a smooth helmet shape read as a wig. The cel
  shadow under the fringe now follows the strands. A translucent glow disc
  behind the book read as a grey plate and was replaced with light-ray strokes.
- **Round 2:** the curl still sinks into the far front lock and disappears in
  silhouette. Push it out past the hair mass, or thin the far lock. Add violet
  up-light on the chin and the underside of the glasses so the tome visibly
  lights her face.

## B: The Skull Stamp ("Specimen")

- **What it does:** "she shows the artifact". Far brow arched, a knowing
  half-smile, the skull-topped stamp raised beside her face toward the text box
  with its violet rubber face showing. The hook emphasis is the ribbon, scaled
  1.6x and lifted so the bow rises as a dark wing above the back of the head.
- **Changed after looking:** the first fist read as a thumbs-up, with the
  handle looking like part of the hand. It now has a wrapped thumb across the
  top and curved finger lines. The ribbon was lifted after the 48 px
  silhouette showed it hidden inside the hair dome.
- **Round 2:** the fist is still the weakest drawing on the sheets. Try a
  pinch grip on the stamp base with the stamp face turned fully to the viewer.
  The enlarged bow is the clearest silhouette hook of the three. Consider
  making it the canon size for every expression.

## C: Quiet Please

- **What it does:** a tighter crop (1.13x) so the face and glasses carry the
  frame. Index finger to the lips, far eye shut in a wink behind the lens,
  violet sparkles. The hook emphasis is the crossed-bone hairpin, enlarged
  1.5x and moved onto the crown edge so the bones break the head outline.
- **Changed after looking:** the finger was redrawn narrow and centred on the
  lips, with a nail mark and a shadowed fist below the chin, because the first
  version read as a thumb. The upper-iris shading was clipped to the iris; at
  this crop it had shown as a dark box over the white of the eye.
- **Round 2:** the bones break the outline at 48 px, but they are small against
  the hair dome. Try them at 2x, or add the canon's small skull charm to the pin.

## Shared observations

- At 128 px all three read as cute and as Libby: lavender mass, round glasses,
  dark collar.
- At 48 px all three silhouettes are a wavy dome with a bow. That suits one
  established character, but the bow and curl are the only above-the-shoulder
  breaks in the outline. For the cast-wide silhouette test, the strongest move
  is B's oversized ribbon, kept across all expressions.
- The robe is dark on the dark #07111a card. It depends on the lavender rim
  light and the trim to separate. Round 2 could widen the rim or add a cooler
  violet to the card ground behind the shoulders.
- Strongest: **B**. It has the clearest silhouette (the ribbon wing), the prop
  ties to her "specimen over cadence" trait, and the stamp's skull keeps the
  canon archive motif in the frame.
