# Void: designer's notes, round 1

Canon is `cotsc-praxis/character-sheets/void-sheet.png`. Kept in all three: a pointed
indigo hood, a face of pure black void with two tall white glowing slits, an orange
scarf, dark gloves, the glowing violet document, and the swirl portal. No drawn face.
Expression comes only from eye shape, hood tilt, the scarf and the hands.

Shared construction: a dome-cone hood with a soft cusp at the peak and a centre seam
running down to the face opening. The opening is onion-shaped: it comes to a point
under the seam and is round at the bottom. It sits right of centre for the
three-quarter turn, and the far eye is narrower. Each material has one shadow tone and
one highlight. A violet rim light sits on the right edge of the hood and robe. The line
colour is `#170c3a`. The only blur is on the eye glow and the document glow.

## Directions

- **A, Classic Hood.** Hood, eyes and scarf, and nothing else. The scarf tail streams
  forward across the lower right of the frame, so the portrait points at the text box.
  The figure is shifted 70 px left to make room for it. This is the cleanest read at
  128 px and the only direction whose silhouette changes, because the streamer
  breaks the hood mound.
- **B, Reading the Rules.** The glowing rulebook is raised to the void with both gloved
  hands, and the eyes peek over its top edge. The hood tilts 5 degrees forward. This
  is the moderator mid-ruling, and the strongest character beat. At 128 px the
  tablet glow pulls focus about as much as the eyes do.
- **C, Unimpressed.** The eyes are lidded: flat tops tilted in, rounded bottoms. The
  hood leans back, the arms are crossed with the cuffs open toward the viewer, and
  the swirl portal sits behind the upper left.

## What changed after looking

1. The first hood was a narrow bullet that read as a thumb. I widened it into a bell
   that drapes onto the shoulders and gave the opening a point under the seam.
2. My second pass overcorrected into a wizard cone. I rounded the upper sides into a
   dome and kept only a soft cusp.
3. A's back tail read as an orange flame stuck to the hood. I removed it and turned
   the front tail into a long forked streamer toward the text box.
4. C's portal sat dead centre behind the hood, so the swirl was hidden and read as
   concentric rings. I moved it to the upper left with two arms of 1.8 turns, so the
   spiral's eye shows. The crossed arms went from two stacked bars to two sleeves
   rising in opposite directions with dark cuff openings, and moved up 26 px out of
   the crop.
5. At 128 px, C's lidded eyes shrank to dots. I made them wider and taller.
6. In B's full body the tablet was drawn under the hood and was invisible. It is now
   drawn on top, over the lower face.

## Checks

- At 128 px (crop of the card), all three read as Void. A reads best, then C
  (the eyes now hold), then B (busy, but the gesture reads).
- At 48 px (filled black), all three are a pointed hood mound. That shape alone should
  be unique in the cast, since nobody else is hooded. A adds the streamer and C a
  short back tail. B's tablet sits inside the hood outline and adds nothing to the
  silhouette.

## Palette collisions (canon wins, flagged for the cast-wide pass)

- The robe indigo `#4b2fa6` and glow `#b892ff` are near Nibu's violet `#9a6bff`. Void
  stays blue-indigo and dark, and Nibu should stay the lighter, redder violet. Run the
  side-by-side check against Nibu.
- The scarf orange `#f2801f` is near Eureka's ember `#ff8a2a` and covers more than 10
  percent of the bust. It is canon and Void's most recognisable colour, so I did not
  change it. If the two collide side by side, shift Eureka's ember or render Void's
  scarf a touch redder, toward `#e8701a`.

## Round 2

- B: enlarge the tablet and let one corner break the hood outline on the text-box
  side, so the silhouette carries the prop.
- C: the portal and the back tail crowd the left. Try C with no back tail, and give
  the portal a stone rim so it reads as the canon door rather than a disc.
- A: try the streamer one band higher, crossing the hood's lower right edge, for more
  sweep.
- All: test a darker ground inside the card, because Void is the one cast member whose
  canon lives on black. The void face currently gets its contrast from the lilac card.
- Expression set: fix the eye line (around y 380) and the shoulder line, and vary the
  eyes only: tall (neutral), flat-lidded (unimpressed), slanted (stern), arcs
  (pleased), one slit (wink or side glance).
