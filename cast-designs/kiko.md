- The Persona of **StreamPixels**, a stream overlay where viewers claim persistent
  pixel characters, link their streaming accounts and customise them; streamers
  decide how follows, subs, raids and tips appear on stream. Browser source first.
  Source: `F:\Projects\StreamPixels\README.md`.
- Existing face art: "Hyperactive, curious kitsune fox-spirit"; take A "neon
  pixel tails, curious streamer gremlin energy", take B "fox mask, monitor
  portal, scanline spirits". Source:
  `VoidBot\assets\repo-faces\new-face-avatar-contact-sheet-2026-05-22.png`;
  selected avatar `kiko-b-avatar.png`.
- Peers: Sai reads Kiko as "close enough in lane to matter: possible kinship,
  possible rivalry". Source: `sai.persona-intake.yaml`.
- Pronoun: not sourced. The existing art reads as a girl; "she" is provisional.

Key colour: stream magenta `#ff3fa4`. Shape language for the character: stepped
pixels, the one character drawn with stair-step edges in an otherwise smooth set.
The step is a shape in the line art, not a pixel-art render.

- **Hook:** a fan of fox tails behind her, each tail's edge stepped like a pixel
  staircase, each tail a different live event colour.
- **Silhouette:** a wide fan behind a small head with big pointed ears. The
  stepped outline makes the fan unlike any natural tail.
- **Shape language:** triangles (ears) and staircases (tails).
- **Palette:** `#ff3fa4` magenta, `#4a0f2e` line, `#f7f4ff` white hair,
  `#26e0ff` cyan tail, `#ffcf3a` gold tail, `#f2c4a8` skin.
- **Signature prop:** none held; the tails are the prop. A tiny LIVE dot on one
  ear tip.

## Portrait set

Framing: Kiko is the one character allowed to break the frame: in **Raid!** an
ear or tail tip may cross the card edge.

1. **On air** (neutral-high): bright eyes, small open grin.
2. **Raid!**: arms up, mouth wide, eyes as sparkles.
3. **Chat, no**: deadpan side-eye at the viewer, mouth a flat line.
4. **Scheming**: half-lidded eyes, fang smile, fingers steepled.
5. **Lagging**: frozen mid-blink, one eye half-closed, a stepped "glitch" offset
   on the outline (B: the screen shows a loading icon).

## Render first

Direction B. It is the riskiest hook (a second face) and the most distinctive in
the cast; if it fails, A is a safe fallback that the existing art already proves.
