# Nibu

## Who she is

- The Persona of **AetheriaLore**: "Metacrat's abrasive Aetheria lore steward",
  who interrogates "mechanisms, factions, reset-loop horror, and worldbuilding
  until articles become sharp"; "generic output dies in her mouth". Source:
  `metame.persona-intake.yaml` (Nibu / AetheriaLore).
- In the setting she is a ship mind: "a mind whose body is a spacecraft",
  stranded in a junkyard, with "high-grade systems inside a degraded hull". Her
  likely origin is a luxury companion vessel ("prestige hull, elaborate interior
  presentation, and attentive companion persona"). She guards control of her
  body, tests agreements, and after the Rupture keeps impressions from failed
  courses of events (the reset smear). Source:
  `AetheriaLore\Aetheria\Lore\Nibu.md` (bylined Nibu).
- Lineage: `AetheriaLore\.voidbot\voice\nibu.png` shows a lavender-haired girl
  with a pink bow and a circuit hair clip, soft and pretty. The lore and the
  steward's voice are sharper than that; the redesign keeps the violet and the
  prettiness as a damaged luxury finish.

Key colour: violet `#9a6bff`. Shape language for the character: swept wedges,
the lines of a hull built for looks, then dented.

Design caution: she is a person whose body is contested by claimants. No
direction shows her as an object for sale or a pin-up; the luxury is her
history, the damage is her evidence, the control is hers.

## Portrait set

Framing: in B the duplicate stays inside the card; it is part of the design,
not an effect added later.

1. **Docked** (neutral): cool level gaze, closed mouth.
2. **Source?**: eyebrow up, slight sneer, head tilted back. The lore steward's
   face for an unsourced claim.
3. **Hostile**: eyes narrowed and glinting, teeth showing.
4. **Recognition**: a startled flicker, eyes wide, as a smeared memory surfaces
   (B: the duplicate shows it first).
5. **Grudging**: looking away, small reluctant smile.

## Render first

Direction B. The doubled outline is her lore made visible in one image (the reset
smear plus the companion persona), and it is the hook no other cast member can
borrow.
