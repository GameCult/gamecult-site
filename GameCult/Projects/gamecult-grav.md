# gamecult-grav

*"The previous public face of the studio, preserved because history is annoying but useful."*

`gamecult-grav` is the legacy Grav site archive. It holds the old public copy, templates, assets, and structural choices from the CMS era before the studio moved the site into Quartz and a saner Git-driven workflow.

## Founding Idea

The founding idea was just "this is the website." Grav, synced content, CMS workflows, the usual arrangement where publishing convenience and structural clarity are always trying to quietly murder each other behind the curtains.

## Trajectory

The public commit history is delightfully unromantic. It is mostly GitSync noise from 2022, which tells you exactly how much of the site's life happened through the CMS rather than through thoughtful repo practice. The one genuinely recent public touch is a 2026 logo-asset restore right before the final migration work. That is the repo confessing that it had already become more archive than living system.

## Ambition

Its ambition now is not to come back. Good. The job of `gamecult-grav` is to preserve the old copy, page structure, and assets long enough for the useful parts to be extracted without pretending the old publishing model still deserves anyone's affection.
