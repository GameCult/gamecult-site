# Weksa proposals: designer's notes

## The brief changed twice while this round was being drawn

- Operator: "Weksa should be covered in books and ciphers." Books and ciphers are
  the shared core of every direction: strapped book stacks, bookmark ribbons, a
  satchel, cipher wheels, glyph cards, a code-stick (scytale), and glyph
  embroidery on the coat's collar band.
- Weksa already has an established design: the cotsc-praxis character sheet
  (`gamecult-site/GameCult/static/interactive/cotsc-praxis/character-sheets/weksa-sheet.png`).
  It shows brown skin, big messy wavy brown hair with a half-up top-knot pinned
  by beaded sticks, round gold glasses, amber eyes, dangling earrings, a cream
  coat with teal embroidered trim, a brown vest and an overflowing satchel of
  scrolls, books and glyph cards, and a glowing cipher tablet. Canon overrides
  `weksa.md`. A, B and C are no longer the speech-balloon, scarf and braid-tree
  reinventions. They are three takes on that design, drawn from the reference,
  each one pushing a different book or cipher prop as the hook.
- The one direction finished before the canon arrived (the speech-balloon hair)
  is kept as `weksa-old-A.svg` / `.png`.

## What all three share

The bust, the face and the coat are canon. The face is a three-quarter view
turned toward the text box. The eyes are amber with a layered iris and two
speculars, and the heavy upper lid ends in a flick at the outer corner. The
glasses are gold rims over a dark under-line. The top-knot is pinned with beaded
sticks, and a teal hair tie holds it. The collar band is teal with cream glyph
stitching. The cel shading uses one shadow tone per material, and the rim light
is terracotta on the far (right) side.

The palette has seven colours, one over the README's limit of six. Canon needs
the teal and the brass, and terracotta stays as the key. Terracotta is used on
beads, book covers, ribbons and the rim light, because canon has no large
terracotta area. Round 2 should decide whether Weksa's key is terracotta at all
or moves to canon teal. If it moves, it has to stay clear of Aqua's cyan.

## A: The Walking Library

- The satchel is the hook. A leather pack behind her near shoulder overflows
  with two scrolls, two books with ribbons and a glyph banner on a brass-topped
  pole. The pack flap carries a cipher wheel. A strap with a brass buckle crosses
  her chest. Her mouth is open in a happy expression.
- **Changed after looking:** the first mane was a spiky lion's mane, and the long
  highlight strokes read as a headband. I tamed the mane to wavy locks and added
  S-curl side locks to break up the bob shape. I raised the banner and splayed the
  scrolls so they stick out of the hair mass, and shifted the bust down so the
  knot no longer crops at the card edge.
- **Round 2:** at 48 px the pack partly merges into the hair. Push the banner and
  scrolls further out to the left with a clear gap, or put a large book spine
  above the shoulder line.

## B: The Cipher Tablet

- Canon's interlingua pose. A glowing teal cipher tablet is raised beside her
  face toward the text box: a wheel ring, a diamond glyph and text lines. Her hand
  grips the lower corner, with a bead bracelet on that wrist. Her earring is a
  cipher-wheel disc, and a quill goes through the top-knot. A red glyph-book is
  tucked on the near side. Her expression is a knowing closed smile, mid-decode.
- **Changed after looking:** the hand first read as a raised finger across the
  screen, so I moved it to grip the corner and shortened the thumb. I shrank the
  glow from a large circle that muddied the hair to an ellipse that hugs the
  tablet. The radial gradient is the sheet's only gradient.
- **Round 2:** this sheet has the strongest silhouette, a square plate beside the
  head. The glow mint is outside the palette and needs a ruling. Try the tablet
  tilted further toward the viewer so its face reads at 128 px.

## C: Pinned With Ciphers

- The hook is in the hair. Four glyph cards fan out behind her ear like a
  kanzashi. A code-stick (a scytale wrapped in a cream strip), a quill and a
  beaded stick go through the top-knot. Two bookmark ribbons, terracotta and
  teal, with notched ends, stream from the knot. A strapped stack of two books
  sits on her far shoulder. Her expression is open delight, the "New word!" read.
- **Changed after looking:** the first card fan was too tight and fused into a
  hump in the silhouette. I spread the fan wider and further out and enlarged the
  cards, so the silhouette shows separate card teeth.
- **Round 2:** the ribbons can read as a red streak at a glance. Add a second
  fork to them or shorten them. If the code-stick and the quill compete for the
  knot, consider dropping the quill.

## Strongest

B reads best at both 128 px and 48 px: one big clean shape on the text-box side,
and the prop is canon. C carries "covered in ciphers" best with the hook
entirely above the shoulders, so it suits a bust product. A is the most canon
(the satchel), but its silhouette is the weakest.
