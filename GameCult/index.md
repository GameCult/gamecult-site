## The Sleeping Colossus

Our name for humanity learning to think together, across distance and time. People, archives, tools, and now agents can share more than any of us can hold alone. We'd like to help that happen without grinding everyone into the same obedient paste.

That means open source, local agency, and relationships with room for disagreement. The people doing the work should own it. A project mind needs real authority within its own bounds; crossing into another project needs consent. Humans remain legally accountable and part of the argument.

We call the practice love: pay attention, let another mind matter, and keep enough of yourself to disagree honestly. [More on the Cult and its praxis](/Blog/love-is-how-the-colossus-wakes).
