# Aqua

## Who she is

- The Persona of **AquaSynth**: the `.aqua` patch DSL parsed into a patch graph
  and emitted as Faust DSP, with DX7 and ZynAddSubFX as reference-parity oracles,
  and Weksa utterance handoff for voice. Source: `F:\Projects\AquaSynth\README.md`.
  Her synth is also the voice of the dings, the audible task notifications the
  root agent plays. Source: `~/.claude/output-styles/eureka-self.md`.
- She is the AquaSynth icon at the top of the README (`AquaSynth\icon.png`): a
  round orange-yellow goldfish with big blue eyes, an open happy mouth, and black
  over-ear headphones with cyan rings, swimming in a glass tank with bubbles,
  music notes and pebbles. The operator, 2026-10-10: "Aqua's right there in the
  readme for AquaSynth, she's adorable".
- "a bubbly Persona whose cuteness must still advocate for real synth needs";
  "warm and delightful, but also as a domain steward who should demand patch
  language, presets, scoring, and reference-synth seriousness." Source:
  `metame.persona-intake.yaml` (Aqua / AquaSynth).

What must survive every direction: **goldfish, headphones with cyan rings,
music in water.** Key colour: tank cyan `#22d3e6`. Body tone: goldfish gold.
Shape language for the character: circles: round body, round bowl, round
bubbles, round ear cups.

Cast note: Aqua's gold body and Eureka's ember orange are neighbours. They
separate by frame key (cyan against ember) and by shape (circles against a
spark); the cast-wide silhouette check must confirm it.

## Portrait set

Framing: in A the "bust" is the fish's head and front fins, same eye line as the
other cast; bubbles may float past the card edge.

1. **Listening** (neutral): eyes soft, one fin or hand on an ear cup.
2. **Ding!**: eyes closed in a big smile, a note bubble popping beside her.
3. **Off-key**: wince, one eye squeezed shut, mouth wobbly; the face for a
   patch that missed reference parity.
4. **Serious about presets**: brows down, cheeks puffed, arms or fins crossed.
   The steward under the cuteness.
5. **Blub**: startled, mouth a perfect O, a single bubble rising.
