## Founding Idea

The founding idea was blunt and overdue: gather the lore, themes, design notes, and fiction into one canonical workspace where the setting can actually be revised, linked, and argued with. Aetheria had too much history to keep living as scattered files, half-remembered Discord conversations, and personal hard drive archaeology.

## Trajectory

The repo begins like a recovery operation. First comes the import and README cleanup. Then the structure gets serious: pre-Elysium factions, timeline work, narrative themes, politics updates, thermal-tech, Cognitum material, and local RAG tooling so the vault becomes searchable instead of merely large.

Very quickly the repo also grows a public skin. Quartz arrives. The front page gets rebuilt. Story metadata gets restored. Folder pages start behaving. Design hierarchies deepen. Continuity mismatches are flagged instead of quietly fermenting. By early May the project has stopped being "an Obsidian folder on GitHub" and turned into a real public-facing lore surface with an editorial stance.

## Ambition

The ambition here is not just preservation. It is canonicalization with enough flexibility to support future game work. AetheriaLore exists so setting, game design, fiction, and public communication can share one source of truth without flattening each other into mush.
