# gamecult-site

*"The current public front door: Quartz, overlays, copy surgery, and an unhealthy amount of header debugging."*

`gamecult-site` is the current public website for GameCult.org. It replaced the old Grav setup with a Quartz-based static site, then proceeded to become the place where studio copy, repo visibility, public navigation, design consistency, and social preview metadata all had to stop hiding behind "we'll clean it up later."

## Founding Idea

The founding idea was simple: kill the old Grav site, keep the content, keep the branding, and move the whole public face of the studio into a statically generated repo that behaves well in Git and does not need a CMS whisperer every time a paragraph changes.

## Trajectory

The public history is wonderfully incriminating. It begins with the migration, then immediately swerves into alias bugs, iframe routing, composite pages, busted layouts, header regressions, breadcrumb repairs, masthead social-link surgery, repo extraction, OG preview fixes, and the ongoing struggle to stop anything on the homepage from duplicating itself out of spite.

This is not a bad sign. It means the repo was forced to become real fast. The site stopped being a content dump and became a living public surface where structure, styling, content voice, navigation, and historical honesty had to coexist without being allowed to quietly rot.

## Ambition

The ambition is a studio front door that can hold the flagship world, the repo atlas, the governance argument, the writing, and the current experiments without looking like five disconnected personal sites duct-taped together. In a just universe that would be normal. On the web, apparently it still counts as a little aspirational.
