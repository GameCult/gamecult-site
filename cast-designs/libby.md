## Portrait set (Persona-style busts)

Framing for all: mid-chest crop, three-quarter facing right, hat or braids fully
inside the frame (the hook may not be cropped; widen the canvas instead).

1. **Shelving** (neutral): level gaze, mouth closed, hands out of frame.
2. **Specimen**: holds the card / string figure / compass up beside her face,
   one eyebrow raised: "here, look". Her signature expression.
3. **Overdue**: flat unimpressed stare, eyes half lidded, a sweat-free
   deadpan; the reaction to a heretek JSON sidecar.
4. **Found it**: eyes wide and bright, open smile, slight lean toward the text
   box.
5. **Quiet please**: finger to lips, one eye closed, small smile.
