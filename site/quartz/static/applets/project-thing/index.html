Executive summary

Renaming Eve to Thing removes IP exposure and unlocks a deity-light Nordic naming moat

Situation

Eve owns the portable semantic interface contract and a seven-repo renderer family. Users love her. Lawyers have questions.

Complication

Eve is based on a copyrighted character. The obvious Nordic alternative, Freyja, would add yet another god to an already god-saturated portfolio.

Resolution

Rename to Thing, from Old Norse þing, the assembly. Everything can have a Thing. Your thing can talk to Thing. Thing renders things.
