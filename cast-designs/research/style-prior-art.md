# Style prior art: Persona portraits, chibi proportions, iconic reads

Facts gathered 2026-10-10 for the cast-designs shortlist. Each line says what the
source states; where no primary source was found, the line says so. Conclusions
drawn from these facts live in `../README.md`, not here.

## Persona series art direction and UI

- Shigenori Soejima has been the Persona series character designer and art
  director since Persona 3 (2006), succeeding Kazuma Kaneko.
  Source: [Wikipedia, Shigenori Soejima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shigenori_Soejima).
- Each mainline game is keyed to one colour: art director Masayoshi Sudou says
  Persona 3's main colour was teal, Persona 4's "a vivid yellow", Persona 5's
  "a crimson red", with black and white text chosen against it.
  Source: [Famitsu interview translated by SegaBits](https://segabits.com/?p=54918).
- Kazuhisa Wada says Persona 5's UI design and red-and-black colouring were chosen
  from the game's theme and message, and contrasts P5's warm colours with
  Persona 3 Reload's cold ones.
  Source: [GamerBraves interview](https://gamerbraves.com/?p=129988).
- Sudou and Soejima met often during Persona 3 to brainstorm the GUI; the same
  look extended to the website.
  Source: [SegaBits / Famitsu](https://segabits.com/?p=54918).
- Persona 5's interface makes heavy use of character portraits and 3D models;
  Joker stays in the menu background and changes pose with the selected item.
  Source: [Mechanics of Magic, Visual Design of Games: Persona 5](https://mechanicsofmagic.com/2022/04/23/visual-design-of-games-persona-5/).
- Persona 3 Reload remade the dialogue portraits and kept characters' signature
  expressions; one cited example is the protagonist's deadpan stare.
  Source: [Yahoo / GamesRadar on Reload portraits](https://sg.news.yahoo.com/persona-3-fans-really-hope-162644075.html),
  [Gematsu, Reload announcement](https://www.gematsu.com/2023/06/persona-3-reload-announced-for-xbox-series-xbox-one-and-pc).
- Not found in a primary source: the exact expression count per character, or a
  written spec for dialogue-portrait framing. What the README says about bust
  framing (head and shoulders, angled toward the text box, one expression per
  line of dialogue) is observed convention across the games, not a cited rule.

## Persona Q: the series' own chibi redesign

- Persona Q (3DS, 2014) redrew the P3 and P4 casts as super-deformed chibi; Soejima
  did the redesigns and said it was his first time working in that style.
  Source: [Wikipedia, Persona Q](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persona_Q:_Shadow_of_the_Labyrinth),
  [Game Informer feature, 2014](https://www.gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2014/07/18/a-glimpse-into-the-mind-of-personas-art-director.aspx).
- His two stated reasons: kinship with Etrian Odyssey's look, and the small 3DS
  screen, where full-proportion designs would look cramped.
  Source: [Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/tag/shigenori-soejima/),
  [Nintenderos (Spanish)](https://www.nintenderos.com/2014/10/el-disenador-de-persona-q-nos-habla-sobre-el-estilo-del-juego/).
- His method, as reported: find what makes each character stand out, then adjust
  those features so they stay recognisable after deformation.
  Source: [Game Informer](https://www.gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2014/07/18/a-glimpse-into-the-mind-of-personas-art-director.aspx).

## Chibi proportions

- Chibi / super-deformed figures are usually 2 to 3 heads tall; guides range up
  to 4, and SD is quoted at 2 to 5 depending on style and age.
  Sources: [Wikipedia, Chibi (style)](https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Q-version),
  [Clip Studio tips, chibi body ratio](https://tips.clip-studio.com/es-es/articles/4806?org=1),
  [intfiction forum thread](https://intfiction.org/t/video-games-of-characters-with-short-proportions/63241).
- Wikipedia puts the SD head at one third to one half of total height and notes
  that details such as jacket folds are dropped in favour of general shapes.
  Source: [Wikipedia, Chibi (style)](https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Q-version).
- Terminology is loose: "SD" names the head-to-body ratio, "chibi" connotes small,
  round and childlike; the terms are used interchangeably. Source: forum
  discussion above (opinion, not a reference work).

## Iconic reads at small size

- Valve's TF2 paper (Mitchell, Francke, Eng, NPAR 2007): the nine classes were
  given distinct silhouettes and remain identifiable as pure silhouettes; the
  silhouette-only test was used during concept work.
  Source: [Illustrative Rendering in Team Fortress 2](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/courses/archive/fall07/cos597B/papers/mitchell-team-fortress.pdf).
- The same paper: body proportions, weapons, and lines made by hats, footwear and
  clothing folds were designed to make each outline unique; interior folds echo
  the outer silhouette.
  Source: same paper; [Valve NPAR07 slides](https://cdn.fastly.steamstatic.com/apps/valve/2007/NPAR07_IllustrativeRenderingInTeamFortress2_Slides.pdf).
- TF2's read hierarchy: team from colour, class from silhouette and shape,
  weapon from the rest of the outline.
  Source: [GDC 2008, Stylization With a Purpose](https://cdn.steamstatic.com/apps/valve/2008/GDC2008_StylizationWithAPurpose_TF2.pdf).
- Practitioner guides (blog-grade, rules of thumb): fill the figure black and view
  it at about 32x32 px; check the whole cast side by side; circles read friendly,
  squares stable, triangles sharp or dangerous; keep 2 to 5 core colours with one
  signature accent; order of work is silhouette, then colour, then detail.
  Sources: [Bugnet, character design basics](https://bugnet.io/blog/character-design-basics-for-indie-devs),
  [Made Good Designs](https://madegooddesigns.com/character-design-basics/).

## Expression sets in portrait-driven games

- Commercial visual-novel sprite packs ship 6 to 33 expressions per character;
  a common core is neutral, smile, sad, angry, surprised, often plus one
  character-specific state. Packs keep framing and baseline identical across
  expressions so the sprite does not jump when it changes.
  Sources: [itch.io Luna collection](https://prometheus-pictures.itch.io/luna-emotional-sprite-collection-33-expressions-for-visual-novels-rpgs-romance),
  [itch.io convenience-store worker set](https://pompack.itch.io/friendly-convenience-store-worker).
  Market practice, not an industry standard.
