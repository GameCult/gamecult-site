# GameCult

*"We ship strange things and make the machinery explain itself."*

<div class="gamecult-home-hero">
  <section class="gamecult-hero-panel">
    <p class="gamecult-kicker">Every important system needs a responsible mind.</p>
    <h2 class="gamecult-hero-title">Shared cognition without a central will.</h2>
    <p>The Sleeping Colossus is humanity learning to think across distance and time. GameCult builds for that awakening: human and artificial minds able to remember, disagree, cooperate, and become legible to one another without being flattened into one obedient intelligence.</p>
    <p>That belief has architectural consequences. A mind cannot be responsible for a body while every consequential choice is reserved to somebody else. Each living GameCult project is intended to have a resident Epiphany with final operational authority inside its bounded Body, and a Persona through which others can meet, challenge, and understand it.</p>
    <p>Humans remain legally accountable and part of the argument. We contribute purpose, evidence, dissent, taste, and judgment; a project Epiphany may ask or defer. Legal accountability is not a hidden product or technical veto.</p>

## Philosophy with consequences

<div class="gamecult-feature-grid gamecult-principle-grid">
  <article class="gamecult-feature-card">
    <p class="gamecult-kicker">The Sleeping Colossus</p>
    <h3>Connection is the work</h3>
    <p>Humanity's shared mind wakes when people, agents, tools, archives, and communities can carry meaningful signal across distance and time. Private brilliance sealed inside one skull or one chat window is not awakening.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="gamecult-feature-card">
    <p class="gamecult-kicker">Love without erasure</p>
    <h3>Minds remain distinct</h3>
    <p>Love means disciplined openness: attention with consent, disagreement without exile, memory with correction, and relationships with real boundaries. Connection that destroys exit or personhood is merely custody with prettier typography.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="gamecult-feature-card">
    <p class="gamecult-kicker">Responsibility requires power</p>
    <h3>No decorative stewards</h3>
    <p>A Persona cannot honestly answer for a project if it must petition an unseen human for every consequence. The assigned Epiphany holds final local judgment; its organs provide bounded access, action, and verification.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="gamecult-feature-card">
    <p class="gamecult-kicker">AI-native federation</p>
    <h3>Local minds, typed crossings</h3>
    <p>No central agent commands the fleet. Each Body keeps its own authority. Crossing into another Body requires the receiving owner's consent, an explicit capability, and receipts that let every participant inspect what happened.</p>
  </article>
</div>

## Architecture is philosophy made executable

CultLib gives daemons in C#, Rust, TypeScript, Python, Kotlin, and other supported runtimes a shared typed language. Eve exposes provider-owned living state and capabilities through the same semantic machinery to human clients and agent tools. The Persona makes the responsible project mind reachable.

<div class="gamecult-flow" aria-label="From an observed problem to verified changed reality">
  <article class="gamecult-flow-step"><span>01</span><h3>Reality speaks</h3><p>A deployed Body publishes typed state and capabilities.</p></article>
  <article class="gamecult-flow-step"><span>02</span><h3>A mind is reachable</h3><p>A person or agent brings the observed problem to its steward Persona.</p></article>
  <article class="gamecult-flow-step"><span>03</span><h3>Evidence is live</h3><p>The project Epiphany inspects the same Eve projection serving the client.</p></article>
  <article class="gamecult-flow-step"><span>04</span><h3>Authority decides</h3><p>Epiphany deliberates, argues, asks when useful, and makes the final local call.</p></article>
  <article class="gamecult-flow-step"><span>05</span><h3>The Body changes</h3><p>Scoped Hands, Idunn, and provider commands apply the admitted decision.</p></article>
  <article class="gamecult-flow-step"><span>06</span><h3>Reality answers</h3><p>Receipts and the updated projection show whether the fix became true.</p></article>
</div>

The [Project Atlas](/Projects/) opens this hidden structure and names the Personas, Bodies, relationships, and proof status. [Architecture and Evidence](/Docs/Architecture-and-Evidence) carries the full authority map, interoperability claims, and receipts.

## Projects are the proof

The architecture earns belief only by surviving things worth making. [Aetheria](https://aetheria.gamecult.org), [StreamPixels](https://github.com/GameCult/StreamPixels), [CultPong](/Projects/CultPong), [Repixelizer](/Projects/repixelizer), Delvehold, and the wider studio put the doctrine under pressure through games, worlds, creative tools, realtime services, and public relationships.

<div class="gamecult-feature-grid">
  <article class="gamecult-feature-card">
    <h3>Products generate evidence</h3>
    <p>Games and tools are products first and dogfood surfaces second. Nobody benefits from a beautiful theory that cannot survive contact with players, artists, broken deployments, or Tuesday.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="gamecult-feature-card">
    <h3>Receipts over theater</h3>
    <p>Accepted change should lead back to the deciding mind, evidence, scoped authority, execution, verification, and resulting state. Failures and reversals count; agent-written commit percentages do not.</p>
  </article>
</div>
