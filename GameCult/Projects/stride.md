# stride

*"An engine fork parked in the org because dependencies have biographies too."*

`stride` is a public fork of the Stride game engine. Like `geometry-script`, it is not a repo whose main story is internal GameCult feature development. Its story is that this engine mattered enough to the studio's technical thinking that somebody wanted a fork under institutional custody rather than leaving the dependency entirely outside the fence.

## Founding Idea

The founding idea was almost certainly technical custody rather than brand ambition. Aetheria and related engine experiments needed a serious rendering/runtime substrate in view, and for a while Stride was clearly part of that conversation.

## Trajectory

The public repo's GameCult-specific trajectory is practically nonexistent. It was forked in June 2021 and never developed meaningfully in public after that. That sounds underwhelming until you remember the repo is interesting precisely because it is a trace of dependency intent, not because it became a community-maintained engine branch.

## Ambition

The ambition here was not to out-Stride Stride. It was to keep a technically significant engine close enough to inspect, patch, or reference if the studio's longer-horizon game work needed it. Sometimes a fork is a build plan. Sometimes it is a bookmark with teeth.
