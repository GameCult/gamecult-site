      <p class="eyebrow">Executive summary</p>
      <h2>Renaming Eve to Thing removes IP exposure and unlocks a deity-light Nordic naming moat</h2>
    </header>
    <div class="scr">
      <div class="scr-row" data-reveal="left"><p class="scr-k">Situation</p><p>Eve owns the portable semantic interface contract and a seven-repo renderer family. Users love her. Lawyers have questions.</p></div>
      <div class="scr-row" data-reveal="left" data-delay="1"><p class="scr-k">Complication</p><p>Eve is based on a copyrighted character. The obvious Nordic alternative, Freyja, would add yet another god to an already god-saturated portfolio.</p></div>
      <div class="scr-row res" data-reveal="left" data-delay="2"><p class="scr-k">Resolution</p><p>Rename to <b>Thing</b>, from Old Norse <i>þing</i>, the assembly. Everything can have a Thing. Your thing can talk to Thing. Thing renders things.</p></div>
    </div>
    <p class="src">Source: GameCult team analysis</p>

        <div class="earrow" aria-hidden="true">→</div>
        <div class="ecard"><p class="eyebrow">Old English</p><p class="w">þing</p><p>The assembly, then the matter brought before it</p></div>
        <div class="earrow" aria-hidden="true">→</div>
        <div class="ecard"><p class="eyebrow">Modern English</p><p class="w">thing</p><p>Anything at all, which is the point</p></div>
        <div class="earrow" aria-hidden="true">→</div>
        <div class="ecard impl"><p class="eyebrow">Implication</p><p>A þing is where parties meet one shared state and submit matters for acceptance. Eve already does this. We are not renaming the product. We are confessing it.</p></div>
