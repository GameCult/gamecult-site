# Weksa

## Who she is

- The Persona of **weksa**, "a procedural language engine for alien grammars
  grounded in concept, culture, and constraint". Meaning is decomposed into an
  ontology, "passed through a worldview, constrained by grammar, shaped by
  phonology, distorted by history", and only then rendered as text. Writing and
  agent intent enter Weksa; typed utterance documents leave for AquaSynth.
  Source: `F:\Projects\weksa\README.md`.
- "Weksa owns structured utterance and language lowering: interlingua, speaker
  state, private interpretation, intended effect, visible action, and trace."
  Read as "the organ that can stop character speech from being raw prompt
  vibes". Source: `metame.persona-intake.yaml` (Weksa).
- Weksa is the first planned second mind (coordinator brief, 2026-10-10).
- Existing face art: "Nerdy anthropologist obsessed with culture and language":
  glasses, field notebook, recorder, glyphs; take B adds phoneme cards, map pins,
  beadwork. Source: `VoidBot\assets\repo-faces\new-face-avatar-contact-sheet-2026-05-22.png`,
  `weksa-b-avatar.png`.
- Pronoun: not sourced; the existing art reads as a woman. "She" is provisional.

Key colour: terracotta `#d9483b`, the red of field-note ink and fired clay
tablets. Shape language for the character: flowing lines and branches, speech
as something that grows and drifts.

- **Hook:** her hair is one big cloud of curls shaped like a speech balloon,
  complete with a tail curl pointing at her mouth.
- **Silhouette:** a large puffy rounded balloon over the head with one pointed
  tail. Instantly a speech bubble in black fill.
- **Shape language:** a cloud outline with one sharp point.
- **Palette:** `#d9483b` terracotta, `#4a1410` line, `#3b2418` dark-brown
  curls, `#f0e2c4` notebook paper, `#2d8a7a` teal-ink accent (under 10 percent),
  `#a8714f` skin.
- **Signature prop:** a field notebook, pencil behind her ear, a glyph sticky
  note on the cover.

## Direction C: Tree of Tongues

- **Hook:** her hair is braided into branching locks that fork like a language
  family tree, beads at every fork, each bead a word that drifted over time.
- **Silhouette:** antler-like branching braids rising and spreading from the
  head. Wide and tall, irregular, alive.
- **Shape language:** branching lines, forks, nodes.
- **Palette:** `#d9483b` terracotta beads, `#4a1410` line, `#2b1a14` near-black
  braids, `#e8c27a` amber beads, `#5f7d4a` moss-green wrap, `#a8714f` skin.
- **Signature prop:** a handheld field recorder with a little windshield.
- **Motif:** diachrony: languages descend, split and drift; history distorts
  every word.
- **Render prompt.** Hypothesis: a branching silhouette separates Weksa from
  every other character's closed shape, at some risk of reading as antlers.

## Portrait set

Framing: in B the scarf exits the card edge on the far side from the text box;
in C the braids may crop at the top, the first fork may not.

1. **Field notes** (neutral): attentive half-smile, eyes on the speaker.
2. **New word!**: eyes huge behind the glasses, hands up, mouth open.
3. **Lost in translation**: head tilted, one eyebrow up, pencil at her lip.
4. **That is not what it means**: flat polite smile, eyes closed, finger raised.
5. **Listening**: eyes closed, recorder or notebook held close, calm.
